# Harvest-month verification log

Verification of the `harvest` column in `data/lookup/crops.csv` (statewide California; any major producing region harvesting in a month counts). Checked 2026-10-12.

## Method

- All 40 rows with a non-empty `harvest` were checked against the sources listed below. Rows with an empty `harvest` (pasture, turf, nursery, young orchards, eucalyptus, mixed classes) were not touched.
- Where a USDA NASS handbook gives California dates, a month counts if the usual harvest window (`Begins` to `Ends`) covers at least 10 days of it. Handbook `Ends` dates more than 2 months past the end of the 'Most Active' period (storage or marketing tails: storage onions Mar 31, fresh fall tomatoes Jan 31) are clipped to the end of the most-active period.
- For other crops the months are the main commercial harvest window stated by the source. Early or late tails from minor cultivars or regions (for example Gumdrop pistachios in August) are excluded and noted.
- Handbook 628 (field crops) and 507 (vegetables) text was read from the NASS/ESMIS PDFs. Other sources were read as web pages or PDFs via search/fetch results.
- Rows marked `approx., unverified`: **none**. All 40 rows have a source that was read. The weakest-sourced row is Wild rice (no single statewide harvest statement found).
- Judgement calls: Strawberries are set year-round from California Strawberry Commission statements even though Handbook 507 lists only Mar 25-Sep 30. Avocados use the union of the regional peak periods published by the Hass Avocado Board.
