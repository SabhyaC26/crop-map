# Crop regions

Generated by `pipeline/06_regions.py` (`make regions`). Output: `site/public/data/regions.json`
(`coarse` markers for z5-8, `fine` for z8-10, plus `search` fly-to targets per crop).

Markers: **26 coarse**, **113 fine**; 135 curated places in `data/lookup/places.csv`.

## Algorithm

1. One representative point (inside the polygon) per field, assigned to H3 cells at res 4 (coarse, ~1,770 km2)
   and res 5 (fine, ~250 km2); acres are summed per (cell, crop).
2. Only featured crops can win a cell: icons `seedling`/`herb` and Native pasture (P4) are excluded; Mixed
   pasture (P3) is excluded at coarse level and competes at x0.5 weight at fine level. Every crop also gets a mild specialty boost
   (largest crop acres / crop acres) ** 0.15 so strawberries or dates can win their home cells over
   almonds/corn. A cell is kept only if the winner's real acres reach
   the threshold (coarse 6,000 ac, fine 3,000 ac).
3. Edge-adjacent cells with the same top crop are merged (connected components over `h3.grid_disk(cell, 1)`).
   Components larger than 4 (coarse) / 4 (fine) cells are split into
   ceil(n / cap) compact chunks with a deterministic k-means over cell centres.
4. Marker = acreage-weighted centroid of the region's fields of that crop; if it falls outside the region's cells
   or farther than 3 km (coarse) / 1 km (fine) from any contributing field, it snaps to the nearest
   contributing field point.
5. Minimum spacing: coarse 45 km, fine 15 km; the smaller of two too-close
   markers is dropped.
6. Coarse diversity (rank order): a crop already topping 2 bigger coarse regions yields to the best alternative
   featured crop (not itself overused) holding >= 35% of the leader's real acres in that region; the marker is
   recomputed for the new crop and spacing is re-enforced.
7. Coarse labels: nearest prominence-1 place within 45 km, else nearest other place within 35 km, else
   `<County> County`; a taken name moves to the next prominent place, then the county, else the smaller marker is dropped (no compass words).
   Fine labels: nearest place within 35 km (valleys count at x0.85 distance), unique per level
   (next-nearest place, else a compass prefix), else `<County> County`.
8. `parent` (fine): the nearest coarse marker among those in the fine marker's res-4 neighbourhood, else the nearest overall.
9. `search`: per crop the densest res-6 cell (~36 km2), centre = acreage-weighted point snapped to a field; zoom 11,
   or 10 for crops that top at least 8 coarse cells (huge contiguous footprints).
